WALK OR WAIT / ROADMAP

Real-time recommendations are not available yet.

Historical delay logs do not say when the next streetcar will arrive. A trustworthy walking recommendation requires two real predictions for the same trip, verified consecutive stops, and a safe pedestrian route. StopStride will not infer these from historical incident counts.

TRANSPARENT ANALYTICS

Every figure comes from a real record.

We publish the source URL, raw-file SHA256 checksum, observation coverage, and definition of each metric. Rebuild the aggregate JSON from TTC's original records using the scripts in the repository.

Unit of analysis: a TTC-recorded streetcar incident. Min Delay is a service-delay measure in the TTC log. It does not give an individual rider's waiting time, the total number of late streetcars, or a per-trip reliability rate.

Null handling: Missing or invalid dates, routes, times, or delay durations are counted in source-quality metrics and omitted from the validated analysis. Zero-minute incidents remain in the event counts; medians of positive delays exclude zeros by definition.

Data provenance: City of Toronto Open Data — TTC Streetcar Delay Data ↗. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto. Independent project, not TTC-endorsed.
